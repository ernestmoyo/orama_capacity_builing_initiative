Our Services

A modular, scalable portfolio designed to serve every major customer segment in the education ecosystem.

🎓
Service 01

Teacher CPD & Up-skilling

Comprehensive Continuous Professional Development modules aligned with Tanzania's National Framework for CPD, covering Competence-Based Curriculum (CBC) pedagogy, digital literacy, 3Rs (Reading, Writing, Arithmetic), classroom management, and subject-specific methodologies.

✓ CBC pedagogy and subject-specific methodologies
✓ Digital literacy and technology integration
✓ Blended model: face-to-face + offline-first digital tools
✓ MoEST-recognised for teacher promotion portfolios
✓ 3Rs focus: Reading, Writing, Arithmetic foundations
🏫
Service 02

School Administration & Leadership Development

Structured programmes for head teachers, deputy heads, and school administrators covering strategic planning, financial management, human resource practices, data-driven decision-making, student welfare systems, and stakeholder engagement.

✓ Strategic planning and institutional management
✓ Financial management and budgeting
✓ SQAS (School Quality Assurance Self-Evaluation) tools
✓ OPRAS implementation and HR best practices
✓ Data-driven decision-making frameworks
🏆
Service 03

International Accreditation Consulting

End-to-end consulting for schools seeking to transition to Cambridge International, Cognia (American), or International Baccalaureate frameworks. Our premium service guides schools from initial readiness to full accreditation and ongoing compliance.

✓ Readiness audits and gap analyses
✓ Teacher credentialing (Cambridge PDQs)
✓ Curriculum mapping and documentation preparation
✓ Mock inspections and compliance reviews
✓ Ongoing post-accreditation support
✅
Service 04

Quality Assurance Systems Implementation

Establishing internal Quality Control and Quality Assurance frameworks within schools and TVET institutions, ensuring compliance with NACTVET requirements and preparing institutions for external evaluations.

✓ Quality Management Plan (QMP) development
✓ QC/QA committee formation and training
✓ NACTVET accreditation renewal preparation
✓ SQAS evaluation readiness
✓ Continuous quality improvement frameworks
⚙
Service 05

TVET & Tourism Training

Industry-aligned Competence-Based Education and Training (CBET) programmes mapped to National Vocational Awards (NVA) standards. Targeting the three priority sectors identified by the National Skills Development Strategy II: tourism, hospitality, and ICT.

✓ NVA-aligned curriculum design
✓ Instructor training for TVET centres
✓ Direct employer upskilling programmes
✓ Tourism & hospitality service excellence
✓ Competency-based short courses with immediate ROI

How We Deliver

Three-Tier Delivery Model

Maximising reach while maintaining quality across diverse geographies and connectivity landscapes.

👥

Face-to-Face Intensive

Multi-day workshops at school sites or regional centres for hands-on training, demonstration lessons, and accreditation preparation. Primary mode for leadership development.

💻

Blended Learning

Structured online modules combined with periodic in-person coaching sessions. Uses an LMS with offline download capability for areas with intermittent connectivity.

📡

Offline-First Digital

Self-paced modules deployed via platforms like Kolibri that synchronise when connectivity is available. Designed for rural CPD delivery at scale.
