Who We Are

Bridging the Capacity Gap in East African Education

Oramah Capacity Building Initiative (OCBI) is a purpose-built education training and consulting company established to close the capacity gap between where Tanzanian schools and training institutions stand today and where international standards demand they should be.

We operate at the intersection of three converging forces: an acute teacher-quality crisis, a fast-growing private-school market seeking international accreditation, and a TVET sector expanding at over 10% annually yet struggling to produce job-ready graduates.
