FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of schools do you work with?

We work with private schools (primary and secondary), public schools through government PPPs, TVET centres, and tourism/hospitality employers. Our services are designed to be modular and scalable for institutions of any size.

How long does the accreditation process take?

Timelines vary by pathway. Cambridge approval typically takes 6-12 months after application. Cognia's continuous improvement model allows schools to begin demonstrating quality improvement immediately, with full accreditation following an Engagement Review. IB candidacy can take 1-3 years.

Do you operate in Zanzibar?

Yes. We maintain a separate legal entity in Zanzibar under the Ministry of Education and Vocational Training (MoEVT) and the Vocational Training Authority (VTA) to comply with the semi-autonomous territory's distinct regulatory framework.

Can you deliver training in areas with poor internet?

Absolutely. Our three-tier delivery model includes offline-first digital tools (such as Kolibri) that work without internet and synchronise when connectivity is available. We also maintain full face-to-face delivery capacity for hands-on workshops.

How do you measure the impact of your programmes?

We use pre/post assessments, classroom observation tools (including Cognia's eleot framework), SQAS scores, accreditation milestone tracking, and where feasible, longitudinal tracking of student outcomes versus control schools.

Are you open to partnerships and investment?

Yes. We actively seek partnerships with government agencies, donor organisations, international education bodies, and impact investors. We welcome conversations about strategic collaboration, co-funding, and investment opportunities.
