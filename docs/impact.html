Impact & Roadmap

Measurable outcomes, transparent financial projections, and a phased path to national scale.

Impact Framework

Measuring What Matters

OCBI is committed to demonstrating measurable impact across four dimensions. We measure success not by courses delivered but by outcomes improved.

🎓

Teacher Competency

Pre- and post-training assessments measuring pedagogical knowledge, classroom practice, and content mastery across all CPD programmes.

Target: 70% achieve at least one competency level improvement
🏫

School Quality

SQAS scores and accreditation milestones for client schools. Tracking improvement trajectories from baseline to target standards.

Target: 80% of clients achieve milestone within contracted timeframe
📚

Student Outcomes

Longitudinal tracking of student performance (NECTA pass rates, Cambridge exam scores) in client schools versus control schools.

Target: Statistically significant improvement within 24 months
📈

System Strengthening

Contribution to national CPD uptake targets, TVET enrolment capacity, and quality assurance compliance rates. Reported annually.

Target: Measurable contribution to ESDP national goals

Funding Strategy

Three-Tier Revenue Architecture

Revenue diversified to reduce dependency on any single source, with clear pathways from immediate to scale.

💰

Tier 1: Immediate Revenue

Fee-for-service contracts with private schools for accreditation consulting and QA implementation. Revenue from Day 1 — requires only standard business registration.

📈

Tier 2: Medium-Term Growth

PPP contracts with PO-RALG and MoEST for public-school CPD delivery, funded through government budgets and donor windows. 6-12 month procurement cycles.

🌐

Tier 3: Scale

Sub-contracting under large donor programmes (World Bank ESPJ-II, GPE, bilateral) for national-scale teacher training. Highest-value, 12-18 month lead time.

Roadmap

Implementation Roadmap 2026–2029

A phased approach that minimises risk while building credibility and regulatory footholds needed to scale.

Phase 1: Foundation & Compliance (2026)

Secure BRELA registration and TIN. Begin NACTVET registration (Provisional status by Q3). Sign pilot contracts with 3-5 private schools in Dar es Salaam. Establish office, recruit core team (6-8 staff). Launch first TVET instructor training in Arusha. Finalise CPD module suite aligned with National Framework.

Phase 2: Expansion & Accreditation (2027)

Achieve Full Registration with NACTVET. Establish Zanzibar legal entity under MoEVT/VTA. Launch NVA-aligned Tourism and Hospitality training. Target tourism employers in Arusha and Zanzibar. Submit first PPP proposal to PO-RALG. Begin Cambridge PDQ delivery. Scale to 15-18 full-time staff plus 20+ associates.

Phase 3: Scale & Digital (2028-2029)

Deploy offline-first EdTech platform for remote CPD delivery at national scale. Secure PO-RALG framework agreement for public-school training. Launch AI-integrated training modules. Expand TVET to ICT, agriculture, construction. Establish regional hubs in Mwanza, Dodoma, Mbeya. Explore franchise model for Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda.

Long-Term Vision

Sustainability & Growth Strategy

Three pillars ensuring long-term viability and growth beyond Tanzania.

🔄

Recurring Revenue

Accreditation requires ongoing compliance support, annual reviews, and periodic reaccreditation. CPD is continuous. These create predictable, recurring revenue streams.

💻

Platform Economics

As the EdTech platform matures, marginal delivery costs decline while reach increases. One offline-capable module serves thousands without proportional cost increases.

🌎

Geographic Expansion

The East African Community — Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda — presents a natural expansion corridor with similar education challenges and compatible regulatory frameworks.

Risk Management

Risk Register & Mitigation

Proactive risk identification and mitigation across all operational dimensions.

Risk Category Description Mitigation Strategy
Financial (Forex) Revenue in TSH vs. costs in USD/EUR for accreditation fees and international partnerships. Price international services in USD. Maintain forex reserves. Hedge where possible.
Regulatory Delay Delays in NACTVET/MoEST approvals; distinct Zanzibar compliance requirements. Engage local counsel early. Phase rollout (Mainland first). Start with non-NACTVET services.
Political/Policy Changes in government education policy or leadership priorities. Maintain cross-spectrum relationships. Align with national plans. Diversify revenue.
Operational Poor internet connectivity hindering EdTech adoption in rural areas. Use offline-first platforms (Kolibri) with periodic sync. Maintain face-to-face capacity.
Funding Concentration Over-reliance on government contracts amidst TSH 3.62T financing gap. Diversify with B2B private revenue and donor-backed projects. Maintain 30%+ private revenue.
Talent Difficulty recruiting trainers with both pedagogical and accreditation expertise. Internal train-the-trainer programme. Competitive compensation. Build associate network.
Reputation Poor outcomes at pilot schools undermining credibility. Rigorous quality management. Select pilot schools carefully. Measure and publish transparently.
