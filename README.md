# Oramah Capacity Building Initiative (OCBI)

**Transforming Education Through Professional Development, International Accreditation, and Vocational Training Excellence**

OCBI is a purpose-built education training and consulting company established to close the capacity gap between where Tanzanian schools and training institutions stand today and where international standards demand they should be.

## What We Do

- **Teacher CPD & Up-skilling** — Continuous Professional Development aligned with Tanzania's National Framework
- **School Leadership Development** — Strategic planning, financial management, and data-driven decision-making for administrators
- **International Accreditation Consulting** — End-to-end support for Cambridge, Cognia (American), and IB pathways
- **Quality Assurance Systems** — QC/QA frameworks, NACTVET compliance, and SQAS evaluation readiness
- **TVET & Tourism Training** — Industry-aligned competency-based programmes for tourism, hospitality, and ICT
- **Education Strategy Consulting** — Policy implementation, M&E frameworks, and data systems integration
- **AI Integration in Education** — Adaptive learning, automated assessment, and early-warning analytics
- **Team Building for Education** — Strengthening collaboration and communication among school staff
