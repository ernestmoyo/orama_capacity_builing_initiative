Customer Segments

Who We Serve

Four distinct segments with different buying dynamics and tailored entry products.

🏛

Public Schools (PO-RALG)

High volume, low budget per school. Procurement is centralised or donor-funded. Focus on equity and foundational literacy. These schools serve the vast majority of Tanzania's 11.3 million primary students and face the most acute teacher quality challenges.

Key challenge: National Pupil-Teacher Ratio of 55:1, with only 23% of teachers receiving CPD.

Entry: Large-scale CPD contracts via PPPs or donor projects
🏫

Private Schools

Representing 11% of primary and 24% of secondary schools. High willingness to pay for differentiation. Seeking Cambridge International or Cognia accreditation status to attract quality-conscious parents demanding international curricula.

Key challenge: Gap between accreditation aspiration and institutional readiness.

Entry: Accreditation readiness audits, Teacher PDQs, QA setup
⚙

TVET Centres

Over 1,483 institutions regulated by NACTVET, comprising 530 Technical Colleges, 898 Vocational Colleges, and 55 Folk Development Colleges. Annual enrolment growth exceeds 10%, yet the gross enrolment ratio sits at just 4.2%.

Key challenge: 30,000 students compete for 6,000 VTC spots; training outputs don't match employer needs.

Entry: NVA-aligned tourism/hospitality curriculum & instructor training
🏝

Tourism & Hospitality Employers

Tanzania's tourism sector is a pillar of the economy with growth exceeding expectations. Employers face documented skill gaps in soft skills, management, and service delivery. Training institution outputs consistently fail to match industry requirements.

Key challenge: Acute skill gaps in soft skills and management; need immediate productivity gains.

Entry: Short competency-based upskilling courses for existing staff

Market Drivers

Key Demand Signals

The converging forces that create urgent demand for OHCD's services.

📋

Teacher Capacity Crisis

A 2020 CAG audit found only 18% of primary and 19% of secondary teachers received in-service training between 2015-2019. The ESDP targets raising CPD uptake from 23% to 50%.

🏆

International Curriculum Demand

Urban parents increasingly demand Cambridge IGCSE, A-Levels, IB, and American systems. Dozens of schools aspire to transition but lack internal expertise.

📈

TVET Expansion

Annual enrolment growth exceeding 10% but gross enrolment ratio of only 4.2%. 30,000 students compete for 6,000 VTC spots. Massive unmet demand.

🌐

Tourism Skills Gap

Tourism sector growth exceeding expectations in 2024, yet documented gaps in soft skills, management, and service delivery persist across the industry.

Competitive Landscape

A Fragmented Market With No Dominant Player

The education training market in Tanzania is fragmented, with no dominant private player offering the full spectrum from CPD through accreditation consulting to TVET curriculum design.

Key actors include international bodies like Cambridge International and Cognia (who provide frameworks but not in-country implementation support), donor-funded projects such as the World Bank's ESPJ and Shule Bora programmes (which are time-bound and sector-specific), and smaller consultancies that lack scale.
